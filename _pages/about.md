Welcome to my academic portfolio! I am currently a Graduate Student at Iowa State University. 

My research focuses on L2 pronunciation, specifically exploring the link between perception and production and how these two modalities are connected. 

### Research Interests
* **L2 Speech Perception & Production:** Examining how non-native sound categories are perceived and produced.
* **Acoustic Phonetics:** Analyzing segmental and suprasegmental features of L2 speech.
* **Pedagogical Applications:** Applying perception-production insights to improve pronunciation instruction.
